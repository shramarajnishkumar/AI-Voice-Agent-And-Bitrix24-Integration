I want to prove that a phone call can flow seamlessly into Bitrix24 without human intervention. Here’s the journey I need running end-to-end:
1. Public phone number receives the call and hands it to the bot.
2. Bot plays a text-to-speech greeting and questions.
3. Caller responds; speech-to-text converts the audio.
4. An NLP layer extracts structured fields such as “desired service”, “company name”, and “contact channel”.
5. The JSON payload is pushed into Bitrix24 as a new lead (or whichever entity you feel is quickest to wire up for the demo).
